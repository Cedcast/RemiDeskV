# RemiDesk Data Processing Agreement (DPA) Template

**Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** March 2026

> This template is provided for enterprise customers who require a formal DPA. Contact privacy@remidesk.com to execute a signed agreement.

---

## Parties

**Data Controller:** [Customer Business Name] ("Controller")  
**Data Processor:** RemiDesk ("Processor")  
**Effective Date:** [Date]

---

## 1. Subject Matter and Duration

The Processor shall process personal data on behalf of the Controller for the purpose of providing the RemiDesk appointment reminder SaaS service as described in the Service Agreement. Processing shall continue for the duration of the active subscription.

---

## 2. Nature and Purpose of Processing

The Processor will:
- Store appointment and client contact data entered by the Controller.
- Send automated SMS and email reminders to the Controller's clients.
- Generate usage analytics reports.
- Process subscription payments (via sub-processors Stripe / PayPal).

---

## 3. Categories of Personal Data

- Business owner contact details (name, email, phone).
- Client contact details (name, email, phone) as entered by the Controller.
- Appointment details (date, time, service type, notes).

---

## 4. Categories of Data Subjects

- Employees or owners of the Controller's business.
- Clients of the Controller's business.

---

## 5. Controller Obligations

The Controller represents and warrants that:
- It has a lawful basis for sharing personal data with the Processor.
- It has provided appropriate privacy notices to data subjects.
- It will notify the Processor of any data subject rights requests within 5 business days.

---

## 6. Processor Obligations

The Processor shall:
- Process personal data only on documented instructions from the Controller.
- Implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures (see Security Standards).
- Not engage new sub-processors without prior written consent of the Controller.
- Assist the Controller in responding to data subject rights requests.
- Delete or return all personal data upon termination of the Service Agreement.
- Notify the Controller without undue delay of any personal data breach.

---

## 7. Sub-Processors

| Sub-Processor | Purpose | Location |
|---------------|---------|---------|
| Stripe, Inc. | Payment processing | USA (SCCs) |
| PayPal, Inc. | Payment processing | USA (SCCs) |
| Twilio, Inc. | SMS delivery | USA (SCCs) |
| SendGrid / Mailgun | Email delivery | USA (SCCs) |

---

## 8. International Transfers

Transfers to sub-processors outside the EEA/UK are governed by Standard Contractual Clauses (Module 2: Controller to Processor) or equivalent transfer mechanism.

---

## 9. Audit Rights

The Controller may request an audit of the Processor's data processing activities upon 30 days' written notice, no more than once per year. Costs of the audit shall be borne by the Controller.

---

## 10. Liability

Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations set out in the Service Agreement.

---

## 11. Term and Termination

This DPA terminates automatically upon termination of the Service Agreement. Obligations of confidentiality and security survive termination.
