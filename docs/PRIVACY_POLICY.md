# RemiDesk Privacy Policy

**Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** March 2026  
**Applies To:** Canada, Australia, United Kingdom

---

## 1. Introduction

RemiDesk ("we", "our", "us") is a B2B appointment reminder SaaS platform. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, share, and protect personal information when you use the RemiDesk service.

By using RemiDesk you agree to this Privacy Policy.

---

## 2. Data We Collect

| Category | Examples |
|----------|---------|
| Business information | Business name, owner name, phone, email, address |
| Appointment details | Dates, times, client names, service type, notes |
| Payment information | Billing plan; card data handled by Stripe/PayPal only |
| Usage analytics | Feature interactions, API call counts, session duration |
| Device / technical | IP address, browser, OS, access timestamps |

---

## 3. How We Use Your Data

- Sending appointment reminder notifications (SMS, email) to your clients.
- Processing subscription payments and issuing invoices.
- Providing, maintaining, and improving the Service.
- Responding to support requests.
- Meeting legal obligations (tax, fraud prevention).

**We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing.**

---

## 4. Third-Party Processors

| Provider | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Stripe / PayPal | Subscription payment processing |
| Twilio | SMS appointment reminders |
| SendGrid / Mailgun | Email appointment reminders |
| APScheduler | Background scheduling of reminder tasks |
| Google Analytics (optional) | Aggregated usage analytics |

---

## 5. Data Security

- HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data in transit.
- AES-256 encryption for data at rest.
- JWT token-based API authentication.
- MFA-protected access to production systems.
- Regular security reviews and prompt patch management.

---

## 6. Cookies

We use essential session cookies for authentication. Optional analytics cookies may be used (e.g. Google Analytics). Non-essential cookies can be disabled in your browser without affecting core functionality.

---

## 7. Data Retention

| Data type | Retention period |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Business & appointment data | Duration of subscription + 30 days post-deletion |
| Billing records | 7 years (legal/tax obligation) |
| Analytics (aggregate) | Indefinite (non-identifiable) |

## 8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction:

- **Access** – Request a copy of your personal data.
- **Correction** – Request correction of inaccurate data.
- **Deletion** – Request deletion (subject to legal retention).
- **Portability** – Receive your data in machine-readable format.
- **Objection / Restriction** – Object to or restrict certain processing.

Contact: **privacy@remidesk.com** | Response: within **30 days**
