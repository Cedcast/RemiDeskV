## 5. International Data Transfers

Data may be transferred to third-party processors outside your country of residence. We ensure adequate protection through:

- Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission / UK ICO.
- Adequacy decisions where applicable.
- Processor-specific data processing agreements (DPAs).

## 7. Incident Response

In the event of a personal data breach:

1. Assess scope and impact within **24 hours**.
2. Notify affected supervisory authority within **72 hours** (GDPR / UK GDPR).
3. Notify affected individuals **without undue delay** if high risk.
4. Document the breach in the internal breach register.
5. Contact: security@remidesk.com
